# P1-17b — Labour Code entitlements: leave, gratuity, bonus and settlement

## Why

Everything P1-16 computes is a monthly deduction. This is the other side: what the company _owes_
an employee and mostly does not know it owes — leave that has accrued, gratuity that has been
building since their fifth year, the bonus the Payment of Bonus Act requires, and the settlement
when they leave. None of it appears in the accounts until somebody resigns and then it is a
negotiation rather than a calculation.

## Scope

- **The holiday calendar** and a **leave ledger** per employee per leave type: opening, accrued,
  taken, encashed, lapsed and closing, with carry-forward capped where the policy caps it.
- **Overtime under the Codes**: double wages beyond nine hours in a day or forty-eight in a week,
  with the quarterly ceiling on how much overtime may be worked at all.
- **Gratuity**: fifteen days' wages for every completed year, on the last drawn wage, after five
  years — one year for a fixed-term employee under the Codes — with service beyond six months
  rounding up and the statutory ceiling applied.
- **Statutory bonus**: eligibility at ₹21,000 of wage, computed on the calculation ceiling rather
  than the actual wage, between the minimum 8.33% and the maximum 20%.
- **Leave encashment** at the daily wage.
- **Full and final settlement**: salary to the last working day, plus encashment, gratuity and
  bonus, less notice-period recovery and advances, posted as one voucher.
- **The registers** the inspector asks for: muster roll, wages, overtime, fines, deductions and
  advances.

## Out of scope

Pension beyond the EPS share P1-16 already computes, superannuation funds, employee stock, and
the actuarial valuation of gratuity under Ind AS 19 (the accrual here is the statutory formula,
which is what a small company provides on; an actuary's number differs and is a year-end job).

## Acceptance

- Gratuity is nil before five completed years and computed from the fifth, with a part-year over
  six months counting as a full year.
- A fixed-term employee qualifies after one year.
- Bonus is on the calculation ceiling, not the actual wage, and is nil above the eligibility
  limit.
- Overtime beyond the daily or the weekly trigger is at double the ordinary rate.
- A settlement's net equals dues less recoveries, and the voucher balances.
- Every query is tenant-scoped.
