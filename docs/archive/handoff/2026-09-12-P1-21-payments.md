# P1-21 — Payment links, gateway reconciliation and subscription billing

## Shipped

- `payments.types.ts`: the `PaymentProvider` seam — create a link, read its state, cancel it,
  verify a callback's signature, read a callback into the one shape the service acts on. Nothing
  outside `providers/` may reach a gateway.
- `providers/fixture.provider.ts`: a gateway without a gateway. It keeps its own idea of what has
  been paid, refuses to pay a link twice or past its amount, charges 2% plus GST on the fee, and
  signs its callbacks the way a real gateway does — so the webhook path a test exercises is the
  live path.
- `providers/signature.ts`: HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body, compared in constant time.
- Per-tenant gateway settings: key id, key secret and webhook secret **encrypted at rest and
  never returned**, the bank and fee ledgers, an expiry, and an **opaque webhook token** so the
  callback URL a tenant hands the gateway names no company.
- Payment links against a sales invoice or a bare amount, with the outstanding balance (net of
  links already out) checked at creation, expiry, cancellation, and a `refresh` that asks the
  gateway what it thinks and posts anything a lost webhook missed.
- Callback handling: verified, written down before it is acted on, idempotent on the gateway's
  event id, and the capture idempotent again on the payment id.
- Posting: a capture writes the invoice's own payment row and goes through
  `postReceiptVouchersForSales`, the same helper a counter receipt uses — bank debited at the
  gross, customer credited. The gateway's cut is a second journal to `PG-FEE-001`, so the bank
  moves by the net.
- Subscription billing: `SubscriptionInvoice` per tenant per period, a platform callback on its
  own secret and its own URL, and `Subscription.status` following the invoice — active on a
  charge, past due on a failure, cancelled on a cancellation.
- One seed ledger (`PG-FEE-001`), routes under `/api/payments` and `/api/platform/billing`,
  migration `20260913060000_payments` with RLS and audit triggers, web
  `/accounts/payment-links` with the link list and the gateway settings, in `e2e/smoke.spec.ts`.
  8 unit tests and 14 integration tests.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **No live Razorpay adapter, deliberately.** No account was supplied, so there is nothing to
  certify against; a guess at a gateway's error codes would look tested and would not be. The
  registry returns an adapter that refuses loudly, and a unit test holds it to that. One file
  replaces it when the keys arrive and nothing else changes.
- **The callback URL carries a token, not a company id.** A URL with a tenant id in it tells
  anyone who sees it how many tenants there are and what one's id is. The token resolves to
  exactly one config, and rotating it kills the old URL at once. An unknown token is refused with
  the same reply as a bad signature, so the URL cannot be used to probe for live tenants.
- **The tenant's gateway and the platform's are separate accounts, secrets and URLs.** Otherwise
  a tenant holding its own webhook secret could sign a message marking its own subscription paid.
- **The receipt is posted at the gross and the fee taken out separately.** Posting the net would
  leave the customer's ledger short by the fee on every collection and the invoice never quite
  paid off.
- **The GST on the gateway's fee sits with the fee, not in input credit.** The credit needs the
  gateway's own monthly tax invoice; a credit taken from a webhook is a credit nobody can
  support in an audit. Booking that invoice is the route to the ITC.
- **A capture is idempotent twice over** — on the event id and on the payment id — because every
  gateway redelivers, and the failure mode is paying an invoice off twice.
- **`refresh` exists because webhooks get lost.** The alternative is somebody typing the receipt
  in by hand, which is how a payment ends up in the books twice.

## Half-done / known gaps

- **The live adapter** (above). The interface and its fixture are done; the adapter is not.
- **No refunds.** A refund needs the gateway's refund API and a reversal that respects P1-09's
  no-deletion rule; neither is here.
- **No payout reconciliation.** A gateway settles many captures in one bank credit. Matching that
  credit against the captures behind it belongs with P1-20's bank reconciliation and needs the
  gateway's settlement file, which needs an account.
- **Nothing sends the link.** There is no email or SMS of a payment link yet; the link is
  displayed and copied by hand. P1-22's messaging is what it will ride on.
- **Subscription invoices are raised by hand**, through the platform API. The monthly job that
  raises them for every active tenant is not written.
- **Nothing gates on PAST_DUE yet.** The status moves; what a past-due tenant may still do is
  P1-01's entitlement question and is unchanged.
- Links cannot be raised from the sales invoice screen itself — only from the payments screen or
  the API. The endpoint takes an invoice id; the button is not on the invoice yet.

## Blocked on you

- A **Razorpay account** (or another gateway) with sandbox keys, for the tenant-side adapter.
- A **platform Razorpay account** with Subscriptions enabled, for billing tenants, plus
  `PLATFORM_GATEWAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` in the deployment environment.
