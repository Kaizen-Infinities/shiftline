# P1-15 — TDS and TCS

## Shipped

- `TdsSection`: the tenant's own rate table, seeded on first read from the standard list (194C,
  194J, 194H, 194I(a), 194I(b), 194Q, 194A, 206C(1), 206C(1H)). Each row carries both numbers —
  `code` is the familiar one every challan and return utility still uses, `actCode` says where
  the Income-tax Act 2025 now puts it (393 for deduction, 394 for collection) — and the tenant
  can edit rates, thresholds and the no-PAN rate, because a Finance Act moves them every year.
- The rate decision (`tdsService.rateFor`), in the order the Act applies it: the threshold first,
  then no valid PAN → 20% (s.206AA), then a payee flagged a non-filer → `max(2 × ordinary, 5%)`
  (s.206AB), then the payee's type rate (a contractor who is an individual or HUF is 1%, anyone
  else 2%). 194Q and 206C(1H) tax only the excess above the annual threshold. The screen can ask
  for the decision without recording it (`POST /api/tds/preview`) so a bill can show it before
  it is saved.
- `TdsDeduction`: one row per bill, payment or collection, with `amountPaid` (the whole amount
  the payee got — what thresholds accumulate against and what the return reports) and
  `taxableAmount` (the part the rate hit; the same number except under 194Q and 206C(1H)).
  Cancelled with a reason, never deleted, and refused once a return has cited it.
- `TdsChallan`: ITNS 281 as the bank stamped it — seven-digit BSR code, serial and date — with
  the deductions it pays for attached. Refuses a challan spanning two months or two quarters, a
  deduction already on one, and a malformed BSR code, because each of those is a correction
  statement later.
- 26Q and 27Q for a quarter (`tds.returns.ts`): grouped by challan, with the warnings that
  actually stop a filing (no TAN, unchallaned deductions, missing PANs, challans that do not add
  up to the deductions), and `GET /api/tds/returns/:form/file` writing the `^`-delimited
  FH/BH/CD/DD/FT text the e-TDS utility imports. Registered on the filing approval gate like
  every other filing; marking it filed moves its deductions to `RETURN_FILED`.
- Form 16A for a payee and year or quarter, reporting what is still unchallaned rather than
  quietly certifying it.
- `Supplier.deducteeType` and `higherRate206AB`; `Company.tan` and `pan`. Routes under
  `/api/tds`; permissions `tds.{view,create,manage,pay,prepare,file}` granted to Owner and
  Accounts. Migration `20260912090000_tds_tcs` with RLS and audit triggers; the three models in
  `AUDITED_MODELS` and the leak sweep. Web `/compliance/tds` with deductions (and the challan
  dialog), challans, the quarterly return and the rate table, in `e2e/smoke.spec.ts`.
  `tds.spec.ts` (8 tests).

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Two amounts, not one.** The first cut stored only the amount the rate was applied to, and
  the second 194Q bill then computed nothing: the threshold was accumulating the taxed excess
  instead of the gross purchases. Both numbers are now stored, and the return reports the gross
  one — which is what the utility asks for.
- **Rates live in the tenant's database, not in the code.** The seed list is a starting point.
  Hard-coding rates would be wrong within a year and would need a deploy to fix.
- **Both section numbering schemes travel together.** Nobody says "393 Table item 2" out loud;
  the challan, the utility and every accountant say 194C. Dropping either would have made the
  screen unusable or the filing wrong.
- **The unchallaned deduction is the headline.** It is the single thing that stops a quarterly
  return, so it is called out on the deductions tab, in the return's warnings and in Form 16A.
- **One challan, one month.** The rejection is deliberate: the department matches money to a
  period, and a mixed challan is discovered at filing time when it is expensive.

## Half-done / known gaps

- Nothing calls `tdsService.deduct` automatically yet. A purchase bill does not offer to deduct,
  and a sale above the 206C(1H) threshold does not offer to collect; the preview endpoint and
  the service are ready for whichever screen wires it first.
- No journal is posted for a deduction or a challan. The `TDS-*` / `TCS-*` ledger codes are on
  the section rows and the accounts seed does not create them yet, so the liability lives only
  in the TDS module. Posting belongs with whichever document ends up raising the deduction.
- 24Q (salary TDS) is not here; it belongs with payroll in P1-16, and the quarterly return
  builder takes a form type so adding it is mostly the deductee block.
- No correction statements, no lower-deduction certificate under s.197, no TRACES upload (no
  credentials yet — the file is downloaded and imported by hand, which is how most tenants will
  work anyway).
- Form 16A is JSON, not the TRACES-issued PDF. It cannot be: only TRACES issues a valid one.
- Sections cannot be edited from the screen yet; the endpoint takes the change
  (`POST /api/tds/sections`) but the table is read-only.
- A non-filer is a flag someone sets on the supplier by hand. The compliance check that would
  set it is a paid API.
