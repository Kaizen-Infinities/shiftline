# P1-12 — e-way bill (FI2)

## Shipped

- `Transporter`, `EwayBill`, `ConsolidatedEwayBill` models; migration `20260907170000_eway_bills`
  (RLS, audit triggers); the three in `AUDITED_MODELS`.
- `modules/eway-bill`: types (NIC v1.03 and generate-by-IRN), fixture and GSP providers, payload
  from invoices (through the IRN payload) and goods dispatches (delivery challan for own use),
  service (generate, Part B, extend, cancel, consolidate, list, expiry sweep), transporter
  service, routes; `gst.eway-bill.expiry` hourly queue.
- Permissions `eway-bill.*` and `transporters.*` in feature `gst-compliance`; Accounts and
  Stores roles. `API_PATHS.ewayBill`, `API_PATHS.transporters`.
- Web: `EwayBillPanel` on the invoice view and dispatch detail, `/compliance/eway-bills` with
  trip sheets, `/settings/transporters`; smoke entries.
- `eway-bill.spec.ts` (5 tests).

## Half-done / known gaps

- Same as e-invoice: the GSP adapter has not met a sandbox; paths and header names are the
  common GSP layout and get pinned in the first sandbox session.
- Distance is entered by hand (the portal fills it when 0 is sent); a pincode distance table
  can come with P1-14.
- No e-way bill printout yet; the number shows on screen. The delivery challan template in
  P1-14 will carry it.
- Consolidated bills are generated only; the portal's "regenerate trip sheet" is not wired.

## Changelog sentence

E-way bills can now be raised from an invoice or a dispatch challan, through the IRN when there
is one, with vehicle updates, extension near expiry, cancellation within 24 hours, trip sheets
for several bills on one vehicle, and a warning 12 hours before a bill lapses.
