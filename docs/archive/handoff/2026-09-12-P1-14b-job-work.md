# P1-14 part B — job work and delivery challans

## Shipped

- `JobWorker` master: code, name, GSTIN (optional and meaningful — an unregistered job worker is
  common and the return asks), state, address, contact and process type. Retired with a flag,
  never deleted.
- `DeliveryChallan` and `DeliveryChallanItem` for goods moving without a sale: `JOB_WORK_OUT`,
  `JOB_WORK_RETURN`, `BRANCH_TRANSFER`, `EXHIBITION`, `LINE_SALES`, `OTHER`. A challan carries no
  tax — nothing is supplied — but it carries a value and the notional tax on it, which is what
  an e-way bill needs. Numbers come from the `JW` series for job work and `DC` for the rest.
- The return clock. A job-work challan sets `returnDueDate` one year out, three for capital
  goods (section 143). A daily pg-boss job (`gst.job-work.return`, 07:45 IST) warns at 30 days,
  7 days and once the goods are late, de-duplicating on the notification link so a daily job
  does not send a daily reminder.
- Receiving: a line is accounted for when returned + consumed + scrapped equals what was sent.
  Scrap is recorded rather than ignored, because scrap left with the job worker is a supply to
  them. More than was sent is refused. The challan's status follows its lines.
- Reports: the job-work register grouped by worker (challans, quantity still out, how many are
  late, value at risk) and ITC-04 for a half-year — table 4 (sent, with whether the worker is
  registered and whether the goods are capital goods) and table 5A (received back, against the
  original challan).
- `GET /api/job-work/challans/:id/pdf`: the document the driver carries, with the e-way bill
  number in a band at the top where a checkpost looks, and the return deadline printed on a
  job-work challan. `?html=true` renders it without the PDF round-trip.
- Routes under `/api/job-work`; permissions `job-work.{view,create,manage,export}` granted to
  Owner, Accounts and Stores. Migration `20260912080000_job_work_challans` with RLS and audit
  triggers; the three models in `AUDITED_MODELS` and the leak sweep. Web `/production/job-work`
  with challans, workers and ITC-04 tabs, in `e2e/smoke.spec.ts`. `jobwork.spec.ts` (6 tests).

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Only job work runs a clock.** A branch transfer or an exhibition has no statutory deadline,
  so `returnDueDate` stays null and nothing warns about it. Treating every challan the same
  would have buried the ones that matter.
- **Scrap is a third outcome, not a rounding difference.** Sent minus returned is not the
  balance: the process consumes material, and what is scrapped may stay with the worker. Both
  are recorded so the line closes honestly and the register does not show phantom stock out.
- **The e-way bill is looked up, not stored on the challan.** A bill is raised from the document
  and can be cancelled and re-raised, so the print asks `eway_bills` for the live one.
- **The register reports value at risk per worker**, because that is the number that makes
  someone chase a challan.

## Half-done / known gaps

- Challans do not move stock. The goods are still in the store as far as inventory is
  concerned; a job-work store or an in-transit location belongs with P1-19 valuation, where
  stock movement and costing are being reworked anyway.
- A return challan is linked to its parent but does not itself update the parent's quantities —
  `receive` does that, and the two are not yet the same action. Raising a return challan and
  then recording the receipt is two steps where it should be one.
- ITC-04 has no portal JSON and is not on the filing gate; the registry entry (`ITC04`) exists
  and the due rule is in the compliance calendar, so wiring it is small.
- No challan cancellation path yet (the columns are there); no e-way bill is raised from a
  challan automatically.
- Tables 5B and 5C of ITC-04 (goods sent from one job worker to another, and goods supplied
  directly from the worker's premises) are not built.

## Changelog sentence

Goods can now go out on a delivery challan — for job work, a branch, an exhibition or a van —
with the one-year return clock tracked, warnings before it runs out, a job-work register, ITC-04
for the half-year, and a printed challan carrying the e-way bill number.
