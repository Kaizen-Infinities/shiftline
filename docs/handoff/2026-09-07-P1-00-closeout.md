## Fixes found along the way

- System-ledger memo was process-wide (a second tenant within five minutes could skip its chart of
  accounts).
- `PUT /companies/:id` flipped `isOnboarded` on every save.
- `rawMaterialService.create` failed under tenancy when a store, category or location was given.
- Leak-suite seed-coverage snapshot flapped depending on which suites shared a worker database.
- Local e2e picked up a stale Vite pre-bundle of `@shiftline/shared`.
