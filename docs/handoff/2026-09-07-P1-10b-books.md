# P1-10 part B — day book, cash book, bank book, cash flow

## Shipped

- `GET /accounts/day-book`, `/accounts/cash-book`, `/accounts/bank-book[/:ledgerId]`
  (`modules/accounts/books.service.ts`, `books.types.ts`), all behind `accounts.view`.
- `GET /accounts/cash-flow` (`cash-flow.service.ts`): indirect method from ledger movements
  classified by system group; reconciles to the trial balance's cash and bank.
- Web: `pages/books/BooksPage.tsx` (three tabs by route) and `pages/books/CashFlowPage.tsx`;
  sidebar entries; `booksService.ts`; two smoke screens.
- `books.spec.ts` (2 tests); routes snapshot and the leak suite's `ledgerId` parameter.

## Half-done / known gaps

- The cash flow classifies depreciation as an add-back only when it is posted to a ledger under
  the Depreciation group and credited to a Fixed Assets ledger; a provision-for-depreciation
  ledger under Provisions would show as working capital instead. Fixed assets (P1-18) should
  settle the convention.
- Interest and tax paid are not shown as separate operating lines yet (Schedule III / AS-3 want
  them); they sit inside net profit and the tax-liability movement.
- No PDF or Excel export of the books; the ledger statement page's print view is the workaround.
- The day book is capped at 5,000 vouchers per query; no paging.
