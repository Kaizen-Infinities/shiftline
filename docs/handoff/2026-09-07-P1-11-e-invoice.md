# P1-11 — e-invoice (FI1)

## Shipped

- `EInvoiceConfig` and `EInvoice` models, migration `20260907160000_e_invoices` (RLS, audit
  trigger on `e_invoices`); `EInvoice` in `AUDITED_MODELS`.
- `modules/e-invoice`: types (IRP schema 1.1), `FixtureProvider`, `GspProvider`, payload builder
  for invoices, credit notes (sales returns) and debit notes (purchase returns), config service
  with encrypted secrets, IRN service (applicability, request, generate, cancel, books guard),
  print block with the QR (`qrcode` dependency), routes; `gst.e-invoice` pg-boss queue.
- Guards in `cancelSalesInvoice`, `cancelSalesReturn`, `cancelPurchaseReturn`: a live IRN must be
  cancelled on the portal first. They read through the transaction client: inside an interactive
  transaction the outer Prisma client does not carry the tenant session settings.
- Permission module `e-invoice`, feature `gst-compliance` (Growth and Enterprise), Accounts role.
- Invoice PDF shows IRN, ack and QR. Web: `EInvoicePanel` on the sales invoice view,
  `/compliance/e-invoices`, `/settings/e-invoice`; smoke entries.
- `e-invoice.spec.ts` (5 tests).
- Fix: `ensurePlans()` now adds registry features to existing plan rows (limits and prices stay
  as edited); before, a feature added to a tier never reached a database seeded earlier, so the
  demo tenant was refused `gst-compliance` on the e2e database.

## Half-done / known gaps

- The GSP adapter has not been run against a sandbox: paths (`/eivital/v1.04/auth`,
  `/eicore/v1.03/Invoice`, `/Invoice/Cancel`) and header names follow the common GSP shape and
  will be pinned per GSP in the first sandbox session. Until then tenants use the fixture
  provider, whose IRNs are not filed anywhere.
- Credit and debit notes get IRNs but their printouts do not yet carry the QR (no PDF template).
- Composition/SEZ/export edge cases (EXPWOP, SEZWOP, IgstOnIntra) are mapped minimally.
- The e-way bill fields on the row are filled only if the portal returns them; P1-12 adds the
  proper flow.

## Changelog sentence

B2B invoices, credit notes and debit notes can now be registered with the e-invoice portal from
the branch GSTIN, print their IRN and QR, and can be cancelled on the portal within 24 hours;
a document with a live IRN cannot be cancelled in the books first.
