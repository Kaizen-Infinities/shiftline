# P1-10 part A — due dates and ageing

## Shipped

- `Supplier.creditDays`, `Customer.overdueInterestRate` (migration `20260907110000_credit_terms`);
  validation and forms for both.
- Sales invoices and GRN bills default their due date from the party's credit days on create and
  update (`modules/accounts/ageing.ts` → `dueDateFrom`).
- `GET /accounts/receivable/ageing`, `GET /accounts/payable/ageing` (`ageing.service.ts`):
  buckets per party, totals, optional overdue interest, per-document drill-down.
- Web: Ageing toggle on the receivable and payable screens (`components/AgeingTable`).
- 3 integration tests; routes and leak snapshots updated.

## Half-done / known gaps

- Ageing is document-based; unapplied receipts and opening balances are not netted against
  buckets (the balances view still shows the ledger position). A reconciliation line
  "unallocated" per party would close that gap.
- Interest is computed for display only; no interest debit note is raised yet (needs the debit
  note flow from P1-08 plus a customer opt-in; leave for P1-10c/statements).
- The CSV imports (P1-00d) do not take `overdueInterestRate` or supplier `creditDays` yet.
- Branch series and the sales-order credit check still read `Customer.creditDays` only.
