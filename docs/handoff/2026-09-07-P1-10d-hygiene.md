# P1-10 part D — accounts hygiene

## Shipped

- Expense claims workflow (`modules/expense`): submit / approve / reject, voucher on approval,
  `expenses.approve` permission, per-route permissions; migration `20260907120000_expense_approval`.
- `/expenses` route and sidebar entry restored; status column and approval buttons on the list.
- `PUT /returns/purchase/:id` header update with voucher re-posting on a new date.
- Cash/bank detection by chart-of-accounts group in the voucher side guard and the bank selector.
- `CancelReasonModal` and `HistoryLink` components; cancel-with-reason on the sales invoice,
  GRN, contra voucher and expense screens; CANCELLED badges.
- `hygiene.spec.ts` (4 tests); the bookkeeping specs create their expenses as Approved.

## Half-done / known gaps

- Sales-return and purchase-return list pages, petty cash and payroll runs still use their old
  delete confirmations (their APIs already cancel); the same modal drops in.
- The expense create form does not expose the Draft / Pending choice explicitly; a claim is a
  Draft until submitted from the list. Claim attachments (the bill image) are not stored.
- Approval is one level; no amount thresholds or a second approver. PC7 (P1-04) generalises this
  for filings, and expense limits per role can reuse it.
- `HistoryLink` deep-links by table name; the audit page shows the filter but the entity picker
  still lists labels, so the picker may not echo the preset value.
