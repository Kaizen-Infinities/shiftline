# P1-13c — GSTR-2B and the Invoice Management System

## Shipped

- Schema: `Gstr2bRecord` (one supplier document in a 2B, unique per
  `[companyId, periodKey, supplierGstin, docType, docNo]`, carrying the split, the portal's own
  ITC-availability call and its reason, the supplier's filing period and date, the raw record,
  and the reconciliation verdict) and `ImsAction` (the company's decision on one record, with
  the reason, who took it, and when it went to the portal). Migration
  `20260912060000_gstr2b_ims` with RLS and the audit trigger; both models in `AUDITED_MODELS`
  and in the leak suite's model sweep.
- `gstr2b.parse.ts`: the portal JSON flattened to one row per document — `b2b`, `b2ba`, `cdnr`,
  `cdnra`, `isd` and `impg` — plus the document-number normalisation everything matches on.
- `gstr2b.service.ts`: import of a downloaded file, pull through a provider, and the
  reconciliation against the purchase register (GRN invoices and expenses with a vendor GSTIN).
  Match on supplier GSTIN and normalised number; date and values then decide `MATCHED` or
  `MISMATCH`, with the differing fields and the size of each gap recorded on the row.
  `MISSING_IN_2B` and `MISSING_IN_BOOKS` are the two one-sided cases. The summary reports the
  portal's credit and the books' credit side by side.
- `ims.service.ts`: one decision at a time, two bulk rules, the pending position with what
  accepting it by default would cost, registration on the filing gate as `IMS` with a hash of
  the decisions, and the push. `sweepAllTenants` warns in the run-up to the 14th.
- `providers/`: `Gstr2bProvider` with a fixture that files the tenant's own purchase register
  back at it with deliberate, deterministic differences, and a GSP adapter for
  `/returns/gstr2b` and `/ims/action` sharing the encrypted `EInvoiceConfig` credentials.
- Routes under `/api/gst-returns`: `gstr2b`, `gstr2b/summary`, `gstr2b/import`, `gstr2b/pull`,
  `ims`, `ims/pending`, `ims/:id/act`, `ims/rule`, `ims/register`, `ims/push`, on the existing
  `gst-returns.{view,prepare,file}` permissions. Paths in `packages/shared/src/api-paths.ts`.
  New pg-boss queue `gst.ims.deadline`, scheduled 08:00 IST daily.
- Tests: `test/unit/gstr2b-parse.spec.ts` (9) and `test/integration/gstr2b.spec.ts` (7).

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Pending is a decision.** The portal accepts an unactioned record by default when it draws
  the 2B, so a `PENDING` row is stored rather than implied, `ims/pending` reports what that
  default would cost in credit, and the warning fires before the 14th rather than after.
  Nothing in this package ever accepts a record on the company's behalf — not the bulk rules,
  which skip anything the portal marks as carrying no credit, and not the job.
- **The fixture 2B disagrees with the books on purpose.** A fixture that matched perfectly would
  exercise none of the package; this one drops roughly one bill in seven, files roughly one in
  five short, and invents one record the company never wrote down. It is deterministic — the
  hash of the bill's own number decides its fate — so tests assert exact figures.
- **Numbers are matched with the noise removed** (case, `/`, `-`, `_`, `.`, and the zeros padding
  the serial) but not more: `INV/001` and `inv-1` are one bill, while `INV/101` and `INV/11`
  stay two, and `A/1` and `B/1` stay two.
- **A document is claimed once.** Two 2B records with the same number cannot both point at the
  same purchase bill; the second is `MISSING_IN_BOOKS`, which is what a duplicate filing is.

## Fixed in passing

`filings.spec.ts` derived its period from the clock
(`2026-${(Date.now()/1000 % 12)+1}`), so roughly one run in twelve it landed on a month another
suite files — GSTR-1 for 2026-08, GSTR-3B for 2026-06 — and failed with `FILING_SUBMITTED` as
if the gate were broken. Adding this package's periods made the collision likely enough to hit.
The suites share one database, so the period now belongs to exactly one of them (`2022-07`).

## Half-done / known gaps

- The credit ledger is still drawn from the books. The reconciliation shows the portal's credit
  against the books' credit, but `ItcEntry` is not rewritten from 2B, because drawing table
  4(A)(5) from what suppliers filed rather than from what the company recorded is a change of
  filing policy — and it is the same question already open with the accountant against P1-13b.
  Ask it once and this becomes a small change either way.
- The GSP adapter has not met a live sandbox: the paths (`/gstapi/v1.0/returns/gstr2b`,
  `/gstapi/v1.0/ims/action`) and header names follow the common GSP layout and must be pinned in
  the first sandbox session, alongside the e-invoice and e-way bill adapters.
- Supplier follow-up on `MISSING_IN_2B` and `MISMATCH` through WhatsApp and email is not wired:
  the reconciliation names the documents per supplier, but nothing sends the message yet. It
  belongs next to the 2B workbench in P1-13d, where a person chooses whom to chase.
- Amendment records are read alongside the originals and supersede them on the unique key. That
  is right for a corrected filing and wrong if a company needs the original preserved for a
  dispute; the raw record survives either way.
- Tolerances (7 days, ₹1) are constants, not per-tenant settings.
- No web screen: the 2B and IMS workbenches are P1-13d.
