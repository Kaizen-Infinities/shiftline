# Shiftline — cinematic trailer (English, 9:16, no voice)

**What it is:** an ERP for Indian factories that puts production, inventory, sales, accounts, GST and payroll on one ledger.
**Angle:** a movie trailer for a factory's books. Title cards build the stakes (every shift, every deadline, every rupee),
each one answered by the real app in a slow, lit close-up, until everything lands on the product's own promise: one ledger.
**Hook:** in the letterboxed dark, a low hit, and "EVERY SHIFT." fades up in wide-tracked capitals under an anamorphic flare.
**Tone:** cinematic, trailer-scale. Letterbox bars, film grain, anamorphic flares, phones tilted in 3D with a
shallow depth of field, light-bar wipes, one beat of silence, then the title.
**Identity:** Shiftline's dark theme (page `#0f1b26`, ink `#e6eef5`, brand `#6d8ff5`, accent `#7c9cff`), deepened to near-black,
with red only where the app itself uses it (the "18 overdue" chip). Inter.
**Source:** the real app at a 432×960 phone viewport (2.5x), dark theme, English, seeded demo tenant.
**Share caption:** Every shift. Every deadline. Every rupee. One ledger. Shiftline is the ERP made for Indian factories, now in beta.
