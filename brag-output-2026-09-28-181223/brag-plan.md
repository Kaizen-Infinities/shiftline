# Shiftline — brag plan (yc-parody, vertical)

**What it is:** an ERP for Indian factories: production, inventory, sales, accounts, GST and payroll, on one ledger.
**Angle:** a deadpan startup launch, played completely straight. Each card makes one flat claim, and every claim is
true of the product, proven by the real app on a phone.
**Hook:** "We built an ERP." A hard cut to "For Indian factories." The flatness is the joke.
**Tone:** yc-parody. White space, big left-aligned Inter, a small "01/05" counter, hard cuts, and no easing on text.
**Identity:** Shiftline light theme (`#eef3f6` page, `#1c3548` ink, `#5b82f6` brand, CTA `#74de7c → #c6f06a`).
**Source:** the real app at a 432×960 phone viewport (2.5x), on the seeded demo tenant.
**Share caption:** We built an ERP for Indian factories. It speaks Tamil, knows your GSTR-1 is late, and will not let you delete an invoice.
