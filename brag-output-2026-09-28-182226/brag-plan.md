# Shiftline — brag plan (visionary launch, vertical, voiceover)

**What it is:** an ERP for Indian factories that puts production, inventory, sales, accounts, GST and payroll on one ledger.
**Angle:** a visionary keynote reveal. Every factory lives in two worlds, the shop floor and the books, and Shiftline
makes them one. That is the product's own promise ("on one ledger"), told at keynote scale.
**Hook:** in the dark, a narrator says "Every factory lives in two worlds." Then two real phones rise: the shift console
("The shop floor.") and the compliance calendar ("And the books.").
**Tone:** premium, visionary, revolutionary. Shiftline's dark theme, deep navy, a slow blue glow behind glass phones,
light sweeps and dips through black between scenes.
**Identity:** the app's dark tokens: page `#0f1b26`, card `#16263a`, ink `#e6eef5`, brand `#6d8ff5`, accent `#7c9cff`,
CTA `#5fcf6e → #b6e85f`, Inter.
**Source:** the real app at a 432×960 phone viewport (2.5x), dark theme, seeded demo tenant.
**Voice:** Piper neural TTS (en_US ryan, high), synthesized offline one phrase at a time, so every phrase lands on its visual.
**Share caption:** Every factory lives in two worlds: the shop floor and the books. Shiftline makes them one, from the operator's shift in Tamil to every statutory date, on one ledger. Made for Indian factories.
