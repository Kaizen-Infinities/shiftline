# Shiftline — Tamil reel (premium, flashy, upbeat; 9:16, no voice)

**What it is:** an ERP for Indian factories whose shop-floor screens speak Tamil: the shift console, hourly
production entry, goods receipt (GRN), dispatch and stock.
**Angle:** "ERP, இப்போது தமிழில்!" (ERP, now in Tamil!). The whole reel is in Tamil and the UI on screen is the real app in Tamil.
**Hook (0–1.9 s):** "ERP" slams in with a flash, then "இப்போது… தமிழில்!" lands word by word on the beat.
**Tone:** premium, flashy and upbeat, built to stop the scroll. The app's dark theme with neon brand blue and CTA lime,
zoom punches, beat flashes and light streaks. Every cut lands on the beat.
**Copy:** apart from the hook, all Tamil on screen is Shiftline's own translation (`apps/web/src/i18n/ta.ts`):
`login.subline` ("அனைத்தும் ஒரே கணக்கேட்டில்."), `shift.subtitle`, `shift.hourlyLog`, `nav.screen.*` titles,
`login.madeForIndia`, `login.gstReady`. The hook line is new and should get a native reader's check.
**Source:** the real app at a 432×960 phone viewport (2.5x), dark theme, Tamil locale, seeded demo tenant.
**Share caption:** ERP, இப்போது தமிழில்! Shiftline-இல் ஷிஃப்ட், மணிநேர உற்பத்தி, GRN, சரக்கு அனுப்புதல், இருப்பு: அனைத்தும் தமிழில், அனைத்தும் ஒரே கணக்கேட்டில்.
