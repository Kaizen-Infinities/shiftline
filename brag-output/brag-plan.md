# Shiftline — brag plan

**What it is:** Shiftline is an ERP for Indian manufacturers: production, inventory, sales,
purchasing, accounts and statutory compliance, on one ledger.
**Who for:** owners, accountants and machine operators in small and mid-size Indian factories.
**What sets it apart:** the shop floor and the books are one system. The same product
that lets an operator start a shift in Tamil also knows the GSTR-1, TDS challan and EPFO due dates.
**Visual hook:** six departments flick past, then collapse into "One ledger."
**Share caption:** Production to payroll, on one ledger. Shiftline is an ERP for Indian factories, with the shop floor in Tamil and every statutory date on one calendar.

**Angle:** one product for the whole factory, from machine to filing.
**Tone:** default (punchy, clean), in Shiftline's light theme: page `#eef3f6`, ink `#1c3548`,
brand `#5b82f6` / accent `#3b62d6`, CTA gradient `#74de7c → #c6f06a`, Inter.
**Source:** the app running locally with the seeded demo tenant, captured at 2x with Playwright:
the login page, the shift console filled in click by click, the same console in Tamil,
the compliance calendar and the dashboard quick actions. Every pixel of UI is real.

**Sound:** a D-major pop loop at 120 bpm (D–A–Bm–G): pad and plucks under the hook, and kick and bass
come in on the reveal. The clicks, key ticks and wipe swells are tuned to the same key and sit under the music.
The last chord rings out under the logo.
